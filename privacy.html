Privacy Policy – Rmndr

Effective October 6, 2026

1. Who is responsible

The Rmndr app (the "app") is developed by Primož Žvanut, who is responsible for processing personal data in the app. Contact: zvanut.primoz@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, ads or analytics.
  • Your reminders, saved places and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Some features send data to Google services to work: the map and place search, understanding reminders and speech (Gemini), and verifying the app's authenticity. Details are in section 5.
  • I do not sell your personal data or share it for advertising.

3. Data on your phone

Stored on your phone:

  • reminders (text, time, place, checklists, notes and attached images),
  • saved places (e.g. "Home") with name and location,
  • personal context you enter yourself so the app understands your reminders better,
  • settings,
  • reminder history and location diagnostics, deleted automatically after 30 days,
  • an error log, if you turn it on in settings.

I do not receive this data. It is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Backups. A backup you create in the app is saved where you choose. If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android may store the app's data in your Google account; this is handled by Google according to your settings.

4. Location and activity

The app uses precise location, including when it is closed (background location), to remind you when you arrive at or leave a place. Location is processed on your phone and I do not receive it. It is sent to Google only as the center of a search when the app looks up stores or places for your reminder (section 5).

To save battery and for activity reminders (e.g. when you start walking or driving), the app uses Google Play services activity recognition, which runs on your phone.

5. Services the app sends data to

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Map, store and place search search text (e.g. "Walmart", "Boston"), coordinates of the search center (your location or a chosen place), language Google Maps Platform (Maps SDK, Places API, Geocoding API)
Understanding reminders (smart and voice input, search) text or speech transcript, date, time, language, names and locations of saved places, personal context the app's server (Google Cloud Functions, EU – Belgium) and the Gemini model (Google)
Reading reminders aloud the text to be read the app's server and Gemini (Google)
Live hands-free conversation microphone audio during the conversation Gemini Live (Google, via Firebase)
Dictation microphone audio the speech recognition service on your phone (usually Google)
App authenticity check device and app data required by Play Integrity Firebase App Check (Google)
Link titles in reminders the link's web address the website the link points to (YouTube for videos)
Calendar (if you turn it on) events from the selected calendars and reminders written to a calendar the calendar on your phone; if it is linked to an account (e.g. Google Calendar), the calendar provider syncs it

The app's server does not store content. If an error occurs, part of the model's response may be written to the Google Cloud system log, where it is kept for up to 30 days. Under the Gemini API terms, Google does not use this content to improve its products; it keeps it for a limited time solely to detect and prevent abuse.

The app does not store the names and addresses of stores returned by Google. Store coordinates are refreshed or deleted after 30 days at the latest.

Google's processing is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Tracking

The app does not track you across other apps or websites. Because there is no such tracking, it does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals.
