Politika zasebnosti – Rmndr

Velja od 6. oktobra 2026

1. Upravljavec

Za obdelavo osebnih podatkov v aplikaciji Rmndr (v nadaljevanju »aplikacija«) je odgovoren razvijalec Primož Žvanut, e-pošta: zvanut.primoz@gmail.com.

2. Na kratko

  • Aplikacija nima uporabniških računov, oglasov in analitike.
  • Opomniki, shranjena mesta in nastavitve so shranjeni na tvojem telefonu.
  • Nekatere funkcije za delovanje pošljejo podatke Googlovim storitvam: zemljevid in iskanje krajev, razumevanje opomnikov in govora (Gemini) ter preverjanje pristnosti aplikacije. Podrobnosti so v točki 5.
  • Podatkov ne prodajam in jih ne uporabljam za oglaševanje.

3. Podatki na tvojem telefonu

Na telefonu se hranijo:

  • opomniki (besedilo, čas, kraj, seznami, zapiski in priložene slike),
  • shranjena mesta (npr. »Doma«) z imenom in lokacijo,
  • osebni kontekst, ki ga vneseš sam, da aplikacija bolje razume tvoje opomnike,
  • nastavitve,
  • zgodovina opomnikov in diagnostični zapisi o lokaciji, ki se samodejno brišejo po 30 dneh,
  • dnevnik napak, če ga vklopiš v nastavitvah.

Teh podatkov ne prejemam. Izbrišejo se, ko jih izbrišeš v aplikaciji ali ko aplikacijo odstraniš.

Varnostne kopije. Varnostno kopijo, ki jo ustvariš v aplikaciji, aplikacija shrani tja, kamor jo izbereš. Če imaš na telefonu vklopljeno Androidovo varnostno kopiranje, lahko Android podatke aplikacije shrani v tvoj Google račun; to ureja Google v skladu s tvojimi nastavitvami.

4. Lokacija in dejavnost

Aplikacija uporablja natančno lokacijo, tudi ko je zaprta (lokacija v ozadju), da te opomni, ko prispeš na kraj ali ga zapustiš. Lokacija se obdeluje na telefonu in je ne prejemam. Googlu se pošlje le kot središče iskanja, kadar aplikacija išče trgovine ali kraje za tvoj opomnik (točka 5).

Za varčevanje z baterijo in za opomnike ob dejavnosti (npr. ko začneš hoditi ali voziti) aplikacija uporablja prepoznavo dejavnosti storitev Google Play, ki se izvaja na telefonu.

6. Pravne podlage

  • Izvajanje storitve, ki jo uporabljaš (člen 6(1)(b) GDPR): opomniki, iskanje krajev, razumevanje vnosa.

Pritožbo lahko vložiš pri Informacijskem pooblaščencu Republike Slovenije, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si.

Privacy Policy – Rmndr

Effective 6 October 2026

1. Controller

The developer Primož Žvanut is responsible for processing personal data in the Rmndr app (the "app"), email: zvanut.primoz@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, ads or analytics.
  • Your reminders, saved places and settings are stored on your phone.
  • Some features send data to Google services to work: the map and place search, understanding reminders and speech (Gemini), and verifying the app's authenticity. Details are in section 5.
  • I do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data on your phone

Stored on your phone:

  • reminders (text, time, place, checklists, notes and attached images),
  • saved places (e.g. "Home") with name and location,
  • personal context you enter yourself so the app understands your reminders better,
  • settings,
  • reminder history and location diagnostics, deleted automatically after 30 days,
  • an error log, if you turn it on in settings.

I do not receive this data. It is deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Backups. A backup you create in the app is saved where you choose. If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Android may store the app's data in your Google account; this is handled by Google according to your settings.

4. Location and activity

The app uses precise location, including when it is closed (background location), to remind you when you arrive at or leave a place. Location is processed on your phone and I do not receive it. It is sent to Google only as the centre of a search when the app looks up shops or places for your reminder (section 5).

To save battery and for activity reminders (e.g. when you start walking or driving), the app uses Google Play services activity recognition, which runs on your phone.

Under the Gemini API terms, Google does not use this content to improve its products; it keeps it for a limited time solely to detect and prevent abuse.

The app does not store the names and addresses of shops returned by Google. Shop coordinates are refreshed or deleted after 30 days at the latest.

Google's processing is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Legal bases

  • Providing the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): reminders, place search, understanding your input.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): permissions for location, microphone, calendar and activity recognition. You can withdraw them at any time in Android settings.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): protection against abuse (App Check) and fixing errors.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, object to processing of and port your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since your data is on your phone, you manage most of it yourself in the app. For questions, write to zvanut.primoz@gmail.com.

You can lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia, Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si, or with the supervisory authority in your country.

9. Children

The app is not intended for children under 15.

10. Changes
